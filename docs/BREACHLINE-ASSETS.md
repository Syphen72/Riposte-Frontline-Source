Breachline is built on the MIT-licensed code of Riposte: Frontline by BenDBSS. `LICENSE` (the MIT notice) and `NOTICE.md` (the
original author's list of what is not under MIT) stay in the repository unchanged, as the MIT License requires. This file tracks
each item of `NOTICE.md`: what Breachline did about it, and what is still to do before a commercial release.

## Still to do

1. **Soldier animations.** Mixamo's terms let the account holder use the animations in a game, not redistribute them. The clips in
   `assets/anim/` were baked from the original author's account. Two ways out:
   - Bake them again from your own free Adobe/Mixamo account: download the Pro Rifle Pack, Pistol Handgun Locomotion Pack and Basic
     Shooter Pack into `anim-source/` (ignored by git), then run `node tools/dev/anim/cuire.mjs`. This keeps the current look.
   - Or delete `assets/anim/clips.bin` and `clips.json`: `build.py` then ships without them and the soldiers fall back to the game's
     procedural poses (`poseSoldier`, `src/06b-soldier.js`). Movement looks stiffer.
2. **Legal pages** (`src/09k-legal.js`). The privacy policy, terms and legal notice still describe the original site: Vercel, Neon
   and Hetzner hosting, accounts, French law. Breachline runs on Render with no accounts. Fill in `LEGAL.name`, `LEGAL.address` and
   `LEGAL.email` (the build warns until you do) and rewrite the hosting and data sections to match. Have them reviewed if Breachline
   becomes a commercial service.
3. **"What's new" history** (`changelog.json`). It still lists Riposte: Frontline's own release notes, including its rename. That is
   accurate history, but you may want to start a Breachline changelog.
4. **New killstreak announcer lines.** The announcer voices were generated with Chatterbox from CC BY reference recordings. New
   killstreaks added by Breachline use the radio chirp instead of a spoken line until new lines are recorded or generated.
